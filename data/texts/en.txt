Every morning the old bus leaves the village before sunrise. The driver knows every passenger by name, and he waits an extra minute when he sees someone running down the dirt road. Children sit at the back and argue about football, while their grandparents talk quietly about the price of water and the weather. When the bus reaches the city, the noise changes completely: cars, music, people selling coffee on every corner. Some passengers go to work in offices, others to the hospital, and a few to the university. In the evening the same bus carries them home again, tired but full of stories. Nobody remembers when this route started, but everyone agrees that the village would feel empty without it. Last year the council promised a new bus with air conditioning. People were happy, but many of them said they would miss the old seats and the driver's loud radio.
