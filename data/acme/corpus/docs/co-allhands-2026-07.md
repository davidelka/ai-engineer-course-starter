# All-hands notes: July 14, 2026

Held in Haifa this time, streamed to Tel Aviv and Berlin. Notes by the Marketing team. The official policies are the source of truth.

## 1. First half of 2026 (Yael Shapira, CEO)

- ARR at the end of June: $21.1M.
- About 1,950 customer organizations on all plans.
- Enterprise is the fastest-growing plan: 38 new Enterprise customers in H1, 14 of them in Germany and Austria.
- Berlin has 13 people. The goal of 25 by the end of the year has been reduced to 18, because of the slower hiring market.

## 2. Incidents and reliability (Amir Katz, CTO)

- Two significant incidents in H1: duplicate scheduled runs in March (SEV2) and the EUR invoice overcharge in June (SEV1). Both postmortems are on Confluence.
- Amir thanked the Berlin support team for catching the June billing problem quickly.
- Reliability target for H2: no SEV1 caused by a change that skipped our pipeline.

## 3. Engineering (Michal Ben-David)

- Kestrel shadow mode started in June with 5% of runs.
- flow-scheduler ownership: the move from Engine to Platform that was discussed in May is postponed to 2027. Engine keeps ownership for now; Platform helps with node and clock issues.
- New SaaS Access Policy v2.2 since June 1: security grants expire after 90 days by default.

## 4. People and Finance updates

- Correction from January (Orly Peretz): the remote work policy that took effect in March allows up to 2 remote days a week, with Tuesday and Wednesday as office days. It is not 3 days, as was said at the January all-hands. Sorry for the confusion.
- The new parental leave policy is in effect since April 1.
- Travel (Avi Mizrahi): the 2026 travel policy took effect on February 1 with higher per diem rates and hotel caps. Visitors to Berlin should use the corporate apartment when it is free.
- New procurement thresholds since April 1.
