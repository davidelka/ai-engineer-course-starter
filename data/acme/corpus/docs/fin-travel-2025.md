# Travel Policy (effective January 2025)

This policy covers business travel abroad and domestic travel between Acme offices. It applies to all employees and to contractors traveling on Acme's behalf.

## Approval

- Every trip abroad needs the approval of the department VP before booking.
- Trips are booked through TripDesk, Acme's travel booking tool. Bookings made elsewhere are reimbursed only if they were cheaper than the TripDesk offer at the time of booking.

## Flights

- Economy class for all flights.
- VP-level employees and above may fly business class on flights longer than 10 hours.
- Book at least 14 days in advance when possible.

## Hotels

- Standard cities: up to $180 per night.
- High-cost cities (London, New York, San Francisco, Zurich, Paris): up to $260 per night.
- Berlin office visits: up to 150 EUR per night.

## Per diem

The per diem covers meals, local transport within the city, and small expenses. It is paid with the salary after the trip, based on the number of days abroad.

| Destination | Per diem per day (USD) |
|---|---|
| Standard cities | $75 |
| High-cost cities (London, New York, San Francisco, Zurich, Paris) | $95 |

The day of departure and the day of return count as full days when the flight leaves before 12:00 or lands after 18:00 local time; otherwise they count as half days.

## Domestic travel

- Trips between Tel Aviv and Haifa: train is preferred. Private car is reimbursed per the Business Expenses Policy.
- Overnight stays in Haifa for workshops: up to 600 NIS per night.

## Insurance

Travel health insurance is purchased centrally by Acme for every approved trip. Do not buy your own.
