# Parental Leave Policy (effective January 2025)

Acme supports employees who become parents through birth, adoption or surrogacy. This policy describes what Acme provides on top of the statutory entitlements. It does not replace the law: where the law grants more, the law applies.

## Birth parent (Israel)

- Under Israeli law, the birth parent is entitled to 26 weeks of maternity leave, of which 15 weeks are paid by the National Insurance Institute (Bituach Leumi) for eligible employees.
- Acme adds 1 extra paid week immediately after the 15 statutory paid weeks, paid at the employee's full base salary.
- The employee's pension and study fund contributions continue during the paid period.

## Non-birth parent (Israel)

- The non-birth parent receives 5 paid days of leave from Acme, to be taken within the first 3 months after the birth or adoption.
- This is in addition to the statutory paternity days.

## Adoption and surrogacy

Adoptive parents and parents through surrogacy receive the same benefits as birth and non-birth parents above, starting from the date the child joins the family.

## Berlin employees

Employees of the Berlin office are covered by German parental leave law (Elternzeit and Elterngeld). Acme provides the same 5 paid days for the non-birth parent on top of the statutory entitlement.

## Process

1. Notify your manager and the People team at least 8 weeks before the expected leave date, or as soon as possible for adoption.
2. Submit the leave request in the HR portal, including the expected start date.
3. The People team will contact you with the forms required by the National Insurance Institute.
