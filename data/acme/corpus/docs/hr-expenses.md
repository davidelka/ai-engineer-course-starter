# Business Expenses and Reimbursement Policy

This policy covers everyday business expenses that employees pay out of pocket and claim back. Travel expenses (flights, hotels, per diem) are covered by the Travel Policy. Purchases from vendors that require a purchase order are covered by the Procurement Policy.

## General principles

- Spend company money as if it were your own. Choose reasonable options, not the cheapest or the most expensive.
- Every expense must have a clear business purpose that you can explain in one sentence.
- Claims are submitted in Spendwise, Acme's expense system, within 60 days of the purchase. Claims submitted later are rejected unless the Controller approves an exception.
- A receipt is required for every expense above 50 NIS (or 15 EUR for Berlin employees). Photos of receipts are accepted.

## Approval

- Claims up to 2,000 NIS are approved by the direct manager.
- Claims above 2,000 NIS also require the approval of the department VP.
- Approved claims submitted by the 20th of the month are reimbursed with that month's salary. Claims approved after the 20th are paid with the following month's salary.

## Meals

- Meals with customers or partners: up to 250 NIS per person, including tip. Write the names of the external participants in the claim.
- Team meals and celebrations: up to 150 NIS per person, and up to four times a year per team. Team meals require the approval of the department VP before the event.
- Alcohol is reimbursed only as part of a meal with customers, and never more than 30% of the meal total.

## Home office and equipment

- The one-time home office allowance of 1,500 NIS (see the Remote Work Policy) is claimed in Spendwise under the category "Home office", within 60 days of the purchase.
- Laptops, monitors and phones are provided by IT and are never bought privately and claimed back.
- Headphones and a keyboard or mouse up to 400 NIS in total per two years may be claimed without IT approval.

## Mobile phone and internet

- Employees who are on an on-call rotation receive a monthly communication allowance of 120 NIS, paid automatically with the salary.
- Other employees may claim up to 60 NIS per month for a mobile plan used for work.

## Car and mileage

- Use of a private car for work trips (not for commuting) is reimbursed at 2.10 NIS per kilometer, plus parking and road tolls with a receipt.
- Taxis are reimbursed for work trips when public transport is not reasonable, and for trips after 22:00.

## Professional development

- Each employee may spend up to 4,000 NIS per year on courses, conferences and books related to their role, with manager approval before the purchase.
- Online subscriptions for learning platforms are bought centrally by the People team; ask in #ask-people.

## What is not reimbursed

Fines (parking or traffic), personal entertainment, gifts to colleagues, airport lounge access, and expenses without a receipt above the receipt threshold.

## Questions

Questions about expenses go to the Finance team in #ask-finance. The policy owner is Keren Amar, Controller.
