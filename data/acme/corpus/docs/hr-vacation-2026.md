# Vacation Policy (effective January 2026)

This policy replaces the January 2024 vacation policy. It applies to all employees employed by Acme Ltd. in Israel. Employees of the Berlin office follow the German addendum, which guarantees at least the statutory minimum under German law and otherwise mirrors this policy.

## Annual allowance

- Full-time employees receive 14 paid vacation days in their first year of employment.
- From the second year, the allowance grows by 1 day per year of service, up to a maximum of 24 days.
- Part-time employees receive vacation days in proportion to their scope of employment.
- Vacation days accrue monthly, so an employee who joins mid-year receives a pro-rated allowance for that first calendar year.

Example: an employee in their fourth year of service receives 17 days (14 days plus 1 day for each of the second, third and fourth years).

## Carry-over

- Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire on March 31.
- Days above the carry-over limit are lost at the end of the year unless the VP People approved an exception in writing, for example because the employee could not take vacation due to reserve duty.

## Requesting vacation

- Vacation requests of 3 days or more must be submitted in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance and approved by the direct manager.
- Shorter requests (1-2 days) can be submitted up to one working day in advance.
- Managers should answer within 3 working days. Unanswered requests are escalated to the manager's manager automatically.
- Vacation during the last two weeks of a quarter for employees in Sales and Customer Support also requires the approval of the department VP.

## Company shutdown week

During the company-wide shutdown week (between Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur), 3 vacation days are deducted automatically. On-call engineers who work during the shutdown week keep those days and may use them later in the year.

## What changed compared to 2024

- First-year allowance increased from 12 to 14 days, and the maximum from 22 to 24 days.
- Carry-over increased from 3 to 5 days, and the expiry date moved from February 28 to March 31.
- The advance notice for requests of 3 days or more increased from 1 week to 2 weeks.
