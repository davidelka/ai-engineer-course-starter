# Procurement Policy (effective April 2026)

This version replaces the January 2025 Procurement Policy. The approval thresholds were raised to reflect the company's growth and to reduce approval delays for routine purchases, and a fourth level was added for very large contracts.

## Scope

Every purchase of goods or services from an external vendor that Acme pays directly. Everyday out-of-pocket expenses are covered by the Business Expenses Policy, and travel by the Travel Policy.

## Approval thresholds

Purchases are approved according to the total contract value for the first year, including VAT:

| Total value (USD) | Approver |
|---|---|
| up to $7,500 | direct manager |
| $7,500 to $40,000 | department VP |
| $40,000 to $150,000 | CFO |
| above $150,000 | CFO and CEO |

Multi-year contracts are approved according to the total value of all years, not the first year only.

## Quotes

- For purchases above $15,000, at least 3 written quotes are required, or a written single-vendor justification approved by the CFO.
- Repeat purchases under an existing contract do not need new quotes.

## Purchase orders

- A purchase order (PO) must exist before the vendor starts work or ships goods. "No PO, no pay": invoices without a PO are returned to the vendor.
- Exceptions (emergencies during an incident, for example) must be approved by the CFO within 2 business days after the fact.

## Software and SaaS vendors

- New software or SaaS vendors that will hold Acme data must pass a vendor security review by the Security team before the contract is signed. The review takes up to 10 business days.
- Vendors that will process customer data must also sign Acme's data processing agreement (DPA), coordinated by Legal.
- After purchase, employee access to the tool is requested according to the SaaS Access Policy.

## Process

1. Submit a purchase request in NetSuite with the vendor, the amount, the business purpose and the quotes.
2. Approvals are collected automatically in NetSuite according to the thresholds.
3. Procurement (Moshe Biton) issues the PO.
4. Invoices are paid net 30 after receipt of goods or services; vendors registered as small businesses in Israel are paid net 15.

## Splitting purchases

Splitting a purchase to avoid a higher approval level is not allowed and is treated as a policy violation.

## Summary of changes from 2025

- Manager threshold raised from $5,000 to $7,500; VP threshold from $25,000 to $40,000.
- New level: above $150,000 requires CFO and CEO.
- Quotes required from $15,000 instead of $10,000.
- New: "No PO, no pay".
