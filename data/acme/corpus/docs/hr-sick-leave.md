# Sick Leave Policy

## Purpose

Employees should stay home when they are sick, recover, and not worry about losing pay. This policy explains how sick days accrue, how they are paid at Acme, and what documentation is required.

## Accrual

- Employees in Israel accrue 1.5 sick days per month of employment, up to 18 days per year, as required by law.
- Unused sick days carry over from year to year up to a maximum accumulated balance of 90 days.
- Sick days are not paid out when an employee leaves the company.

## Pay

Israeli law allows the employer to leave the first sick day unpaid and pay 50% for the second and third days. Acme does not use this option: Acme pays 100% of the salary from the first sick day, as long as the employee has a positive sick-day balance.

## Documentation

- For an absence of 1 or 2 consecutive days, a self-declaration in the HR portal is enough.
- A medical certificate is required for absences of 3 or more consecutive days. Upload it to the HR portal within 7 days of returning to work.
- Employees may use self-declaration for up to 6 days per calendar year in total; after that, a medical certificate is required for every absence.

## Caring for family members

- Employees may use up to 8 of their sick days per year to care for a sick child under 16.
- Up to 6 days per year may be used to care for a sick spouse or parent.
- Single parents may use up to 16 days per year to care for a sick child.

## Long illness

If an illness lasts longer than the employee's accumulated balance, the People team will help with a claim to the long-term disability insurance included in the pension plan. Contact Sivan Elbaz as early as possible.

## Berlin employees

Berlin employees follow German law (Entgeltfortzahlung im Krankheitsfall): full salary for up to 6 weeks per illness, and a doctor's certificate (Arbeitsunfähigkeitsbescheinigung) from the fourth calendar day of illness. Berlin employees report sick to their manager and to Katrin Vogel, the Berlin office manager.

## Notifying your manager

Notify your direct manager before the start of your working day, by Slack or phone. On-call engineers who become sick during their shift must also hand over the pager to the secondary on-call.
