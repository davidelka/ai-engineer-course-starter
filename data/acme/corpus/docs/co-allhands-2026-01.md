# All-hands notes: January 13, 2026

Held in Tel Aviv, streamed to Haifa and Berlin. Notes taken by the Marketing team for employees who could not attend. These notes summarize what was said in the meeting; the official policies are the source of truth.

## 1. 2025 in numbers (Yael Shapira, CEO)

- ARR at the end of 2025: $18.4M, up from $14.9M at the end of 2024.
- Customers: 1,870 paying and free organizations, of which 610 are on paid plans.
- Net revenue retention: 112%.
- Headcount grew from 96 to 114 during 2025.
- Tidewater went live in October: EU customers can keep their data in Frankfurt. 48 customers chose EU residency so far.

## 2. Plans for 2026 (Yael)

- Target ARR for the end of 2026: $23M.
- Grow the Berlin office to 25 people by the end of 2026 (Sales EMEA and Customer Support EMEA).
- New pricing took effect on January 1 (Daniel will send details to customer-facing teams).

## 3. Engineering (Amir Katz, CTO, and Michal Ben-David)

- Kestrel kicked off: the rewrite of the execution layer of the engine. First milestone is shadow mode in mid-2026.
- Reliability: 2025 ended with 99.96% average monthly availability for flow-api.
- The Orca migration is fully done; the last services moved in November 2025.

## 4. People updates (Orly Peretz, VP People)

- New vacation policy since January 1: 14 days in the first year, carry-over of up to 5 days.
- New parental leave policy from April: more paid weeks for the birth parent and 15 paid days for the non-birth parent.
- Remote work: Orly said that, starting in March, employees will be able to work remotely up to 3 days a week, with one shared office day. The final policy will be published in February.
- Engagement survey results: 78% participation; flexibility was the top request.

## 5. Q&A (selected)

- **Q: Will the travel per diem be updated? Prices went up a lot.** A (Avi Mizrahi): Yes, a new travel policy is coming in February with higher per diem rates.
- **Q: When will Kestrel replace the engine completely?** A (Michal): Not before 2027.
- **Q: Will Haifa get a bigger office?** A (Yael): We are looking at options for 2027; for now, Haifa has enough desks.
