# Remote Work Policy (effective March 2026)

This policy replaces the January 2025 remote work policy. It was shaped by the 2025 engagement survey, in which 71% of employees asked for more flexibility while managers asked for predictable shared office days.

## The rules

- Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week. Tuesday and Wednesday are office days for all teams.
- Remote work from abroad requires written approval from the VP of the department and is limited to 10 working days per year.
- Acme provides a one-time home office allowance of 1,500 NIS, claimed through the expense system within 60 days of the purchase.
- Employees must be reachable on Slack during core hours, 10:00 to 16:00 Israel time.

## Berlin office

Berlin employees follow the same rule of up to 2 remote days per week. Their shared office days are Tuesday and Thursday, so that they overlap with Tel Aviv on Tuesday.

## Exceptions

- Employees returning from parental leave, employees with a medical recommendation, and employees after long reserve duty may agree on a temporary arrangement with more remote days, approved by the department VP and the People team.
- Customer-facing roles may be asked to be in the office on specific days for customer visits.

## Working from abroad

Requests to work from abroad must be submitted at least 3 weeks in advance. The employee is responsible for being reachable during core hours in Israel time, and the request must be checked by Finance for tax implications if it exceeds 5 consecutive working days.

## Equipment

Employees who work from home should use the company laptop only, on a private and password-protected network. Public Wi-Fi requires the Acme VPN (see the Laptop Policy).
