# New Hire Laptop FAQ

A quick FAQ for new employees, put together by the IT team. The official rules are in the Laptop and Endpoint Policy.

**Which laptop will I get?**
Engineers, data analysts and designers receive a MacBook Pro 14-inch with an Apple M-series chip, 32 GB of memory and 1 TB of storage. All other employees receive a MacBook Air 13-inch with 16 GB of memory and 512 GB of storage.

**When will I get a new laptop?**
Laptops are replaced every 3 years. IT contacts you a month before the refresh date.

**Why is my laptop so locked down?**
Every Acme laptop is enrolled in Jamf (for macOS) or Intune (for Windows). Full-disk encryption, automatic OS updates, the endpoint protection agent, a 5-minute screen lock and the firewall are mandatory. Laptops that are not compliant for more than 7 days lose access to Okta-protected applications.

**Can my kids use the laptop for homework?**
No. Family members may not use the work laptop.

**I'm working from a café. Anything special?**
On public Wi-Fi, connect to the Acme VPN (Tailscale) before accessing internal systems.

**I lost my laptop. What now?**
Report a lost or stolen laptop to IT (it@acme.example) and to Security (security@acme.example) within 1 hour of noticing. IT will lock and wipe it remotely. You will not be blamed for reporting.

**Can I get a second monitor?**
You receive one external monitor for the office desk and one for home.

**Who do I ask?**
Post in #it-help or email it@acme.example.
