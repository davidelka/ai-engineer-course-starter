# Acme Office Locations

Acme has three offices. Office codes (TLV-1, HFA-1, BER-1) are used in the room booking system, in shipping addresses for equipment and in the HR portal.

## Tel Aviv (TLV-1), headquarters

- Address: 9 Ha'Ta'asiya Street, floors 6 and 7, Tel Aviv. Reception is on floor 6.
- Desks: 70 desks plus 8 meeting rooms and 4 phone booths.
- Teams: leadership, Sales Israel and Americas, Customer Support Israel and Americas, Finance, People, Marketing, Product, Design, IT, Security, and most of the Integrations, Billing and Frontend teams.
- Office manager: Adi Navon.
- Opening hours: Sunday to Thursday, 07:00 to 21:00. Badge access 24/7 for employees.
- Getting there: 6 minutes' walk from the Light Rail Red Line. Parking is limited; see the parking procedure (in Hebrew).

## Haifa (HFA-1)

- Address: Haifa Bay Tech Park, Building C, floor 3, Haifa.
- Desks: 45 desks plus 4 meeting rooms.
- Teams: most of Engineering (Core API, Engine, Identity, Data, Platform) and the VP Engineering.
- Office manager: Rotem Shalev.
- Opening hours: Sunday to Thursday, 07:30 to 20:00.
- Getting there: an Acme shuttle runs from the Hof HaCarmel train station in the morning; parking in the tech park is free. Details are in the Haifa office guide (in Hebrew).

## Berlin (BER-1)

- Address: Friedrichstraße 120, 4th floor, 10117 Berlin.
- Desks: 20 desks plus 2 meeting rooms.
- Teams: Sales EMEA, Customer Support EMEA.
- Site lead: Lena Hoffmann. Office manager: Katrin Vogel.
- Opening hours: Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 19:00.
- Note: Berlin works Monday to Friday, while the Israeli offices work Sunday to Thursday. Plan cross-office meetings on Monday to Thursday.

## Shared office days

Tuesday and Wednesday are office days for all teams in Israel. Berlin's shared office days are Tuesday and Thursday.

## Visitors

Visitors are registered in advance in the room booking system by their host and receive a guest badge at reception. Visitors must be accompanied at all times outside meeting rooms. Guest Wi-Fi: Acme-Guest.

## Emergencies

Each office has trained first-aid and evacuation coordinators listed at reception. In Israel, the protected space (mamad) on each floor is marked with green signs; follow Home Front Command instructions.
