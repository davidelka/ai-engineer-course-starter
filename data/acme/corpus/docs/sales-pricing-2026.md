# Acme Flow Price List (effective January 1, 2026)

Prices are in US dollars per user per month, excluding VAT. Customers in the EU are invoiced in EUR at the rate published by Finance each quarter; customers in Israel may be invoiced in ILS. Existing customers keep their 2025 prices until their next renewal date after March 31, 2026.

## Plans

| Plan | Price per user/month (annual billing) | Price per user/month (monthly billing) | Included runs per month | Users |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $0 | $0 | 1,500 | up to 3 |
| Team | $12 | $15 | 15,000 | 3-100 |
| Business | $29 | $35 | 150,000 | 10-500 |
| Enterprise | custom | custom | unlimited (fair use) | from 50 |

## Overage

| Plan | Price per additional 1,000 runs |
|---|---|
| Starter | not available (upgrade required) |
| Team | $8 |
| Business | $6 |
| Enterprise | included (fair use) |

Starter workspaces stop running workflows when the monthly limit is reached, until the next month or an upgrade.

## Limits and features by plan

| Feature | Starter | Team | Business | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Active workflows | 5 | unlimited | unlimited | unlimited |
| API rate limit (requests/min) | 60 | 300 | 1,000 | 5,000 |
| SAML single sign-on | no | no | yes | yes |
| Audit log retention | none | none | 1 year | 3 years |
| EU data residency | no | no | add-on, $4 per user/month | included |
| Uptime SLA | none | none | 99.9% | 99.95% |
| Support | community | email | priority | priority + named CSM |

## Add-ons

- EU data residency for Business: $4 per user per month.
- Additional sandbox workspace (Business and Enterprise): $200 per month.
- Premium connectors (SAP, Oracle NetSuite, Workday): $150 per connector per month on Team and Business; included in Enterprise.

## Discounts

- Non-profit organizations and schools: 40% off Team and Business.
- Annual prepayment is already reflected in the annual price.
- Multi-year contracts (Enterprise only): up to 12% off, approved by the VP Sales. Discounts above 12% require CFO approval.

## Changes from 2025

- Team price increased from $10 to $12 (annual) and from $12 to $15 (monthly); included runs from 10,000 to 15,000.
- Business price increased from $25 to $29 (annual) and from $30 to $35 (monthly); included runs from 100,000 to 150,000.
- Overage became cheaper: Team from $10 to $8 and Business from $7 to $6 per 1,000 runs.
- Enterprise uptime SLA increased to 99.95%.
