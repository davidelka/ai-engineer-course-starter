# Refund Policy

This policy tells Customer Support agents when a customer can receive a refund, who approves it, and how it is processed. The customer-facing summary is published on the website; this internal version adds approval limits and processing details.

## Monthly plans

- Customers on monthly billing can cancel at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month.
- There are no refunds for partial months.

## Annual plans

- A customer may cancel an annual plan and receive a full refund within 14 days of the initial purchase.
- After 14 days, annual plans are not refundable. The customer can turn off auto-renewal, or downgrade at the renewal date.
- Seats added during an annual term are charged pro rata and are not refundable.

## Billing errors

Billing errors caused by Acme are always refunded in full, regardless of the plan or the time that has passed, as soon as the error is confirmed by the Billing team.

## SLA credits are not refunds

Service credits under the SLA are applied to the next invoice. They are not paid out as cash refunds, even when a customer cancels.

## Goodwill credits

For a bad experience that is not covered by the SLA (for example a bug that affected a specific customer), support managers may offer a goodwill credit of up to one month's fee. Goodwill credits are applied to the next invoice.

## Approval limits

- Up to $1,000: the support manager (Nir Golan for Israel and the Americas, Jonas Becker for EMEA).
- From $1,000 to $10,000: the VP Customer Support, Shira Levin.
- Above $10,000: the CFO, Avi Mizrahi.

## Processing

1. The agent opens a refund request in Zendesk with the reason, the invoice number, and the amount.
2. After approval, Billing issues a credit note and the refund through the payment provider.
3. Card refunds appear within 5-10 business days. Bank transfer refunds are made within 14 days.

## What agents must never do

- Promise a refund before the approval.
- Ask a customer for their password, full card number, or any login credentials. Acme never needs them to process a refund.
- Refund to a different card or bank account than the one that was charged.
