# Vacation Policy (effective January 2024)

This policy describes how paid vacation works at Acme Ltd. for employees employed in Israel. Employees of the Berlin office follow the German addendum maintained by the People team in Berlin.

## Annual allowance

- Full-time employees receive 12 paid vacation days in their first year of employment.
- From the second year, the allowance grows by 1 day per year of service, up to a maximum of 22 days.
- Part-time employees receive vacation days in proportion to their scope of employment. For example, an employee working 80% receives 80% of the full-time allowance, rounded up to the nearest half day.
- Vacation days accrue monthly. An employee who joins in the middle of the year receives a pro-rated allowance for that year.

## Carry-over

- Up to 3 unused days may be carried over to the next calendar year.
- Carried-over days expire on February 28 of the following year. Days that are not used by then are lost and are not paid out.
- Exceptions require written approval from the VP People.

## Requesting vacation

- Vacation requests of 3 days or more must be submitted in the HR portal at least 1 week in advance and approved by the direct manager.
- Requests of 1-2 days can be submitted up to one working day in advance.
- Managers should respond to a request within 3 working days. A request that was not answered within that time is escalated automatically to the manager's manager.

## Company shutdown week

During the company-wide shutdown week (between Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur), 3 vacation days are deducted automatically. Employees who do not have enough days will go into a negative balance, which is offset against future accruals.

## Leaving the company

Unused vacation days are paid out on termination according to Israeli law, based on the employee's last base salary.
