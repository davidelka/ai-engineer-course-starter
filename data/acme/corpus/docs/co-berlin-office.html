Welcome to the Berlin office (BER-1)

Last updated: September 10, 2026 · Page owner: Katrin Vogel

Heads-up: the kitchen on the 4th floor is being renovated from November 2 to November 13, 2026. Coffee and lunch are available in the shared lounge on the ground floor.

Address and access

Friedrichstraße 120, 4th floor, 10117 Berlin. The nearest U-Bahn station is Stadtmitte. The building is open Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 19:00. Employees receive a building badge from Katrin Vogel on their first day; the code for the office door is published every month in Pulse under "Office info".

Who is here

The Berlin office is home to the Sales EMEA team, led by Lena Hoffmann, who is also the site lead, and the Customer Support EMEA team, led by Jonas Becker. Katrin Vogel is the office manager and the contact for anything related to the office, equipment returns and sick reports.

Desks and rooms

The office has 20 desks, two meeting rooms ("Spree" for 8 people and "Havel" for 4 people) and a phone booth. Desks are not assigned; book a desk in the room booking system if you are visiting from Israel. Shared office days in Berlin are Tuesday and Thursday.

Visiting from Israel

Employees traveling to Berlin for work should first check whether the Acme corporate apartment is free: it is a two-bedroom apartment ten minutes' walk from the office, booked through Katrin Vogel. The Travel Policy explains when the apartment must be used instead of a hotel. Remember that Berlin works Monday to Friday, so Friday is a working day here and Sunday is not.

Local holidays

The Berlin office follows Berlin public holidays (for example German Unity Day on October 3 and International Women's Day on March 8, which is a public holiday in Berlin) and not Israeli holidays. Customer Support EMEA keeps a minimal staffing on Israeli holidays that are working days in Germany.

Contacts

  • Office manager: Katrin Vogel
  • Site lead: Lena Hoffmann
  • IT support: #it-help (remote support Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 Berlin time)
