IT Helpdesk

Last updated: May 1, 2026 · Page owner: Yossi Dahan

How to get help

The fastest way to get help is to open a ticket in Fixit or post in the #it-help Slack channel. Please do not send direct messages to individual IT team members: tickets make sure nothing gets lost when someone is out of office.

  • Slack: #it-help
  • Email: it@acme.example (creates a Fixit ticket automatically)
  • Phone: extension 5000, or +972-3-555-0150 from outside
  • Walk-in: the IT corner on floor 6 in Tel Aviv

Opening hours

The Tel Aviv helpdesk is staffed Sunday to Thursday, 08:30 to 18:00 Israel time. The Haifa office has an IT team member on site on Mondays and Wednesdays. Berlin employees are supported remotely Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 Berlin time, by the Tel Aviv team and by an external partner on Fridays.

Outside opening hours, only urgent issues that block production work or security incidents are handled. For a security incident (lost laptop, suspicious login, phishing you clicked), use #sec-incidents and do not wait for the helpdesk.

Response targets

  • Critical (you cannot work at all, for example a broken laptop or locked account): first response within 1 hour during opening hours.
  • Normal (something is broken but you can work around it): first response within 4 working hours.
  • Requests (new software, a new monitor, a new mouse): within 2 working days.

Access to SaaS tools is not handled by the helpdesk. Request it in #access-requests; the access bot routes your request according to the SaaS Access Policy.

Common self-service tasks

  • Reset your Okta password: use the "Forgot password" link on the Okta login page. You will need Okta Verify or your security key.
  • Install approved software: open Self Service (macOS) and choose from the catalog.
  • Guest Wi-Fi: the network is Acme-Guest; the daily password is shown on the reception screen.
  • Printers: print to "Acme-Print" and release the job with your badge.
