# Acme Org Chart Summary (October 2026)

Acme Ltd. has 120 employees in three offices: 79 in Tel Aviv (headquarters), 28 in Haifa, and 13 in Berlin. The full, always-current org chart lives in the HR portal; this page is a summary updated at the start of each quarter.

## Leadership team

- **Yael Shapira**, CEO (Tel Aviv)
- **Amir Katz**, CTO (Tel Aviv), reports to the CEO
- **Michal Ben-David**, VP Engineering (Haifa), reports to the CTO
- **Noam Friedman**, VP Product (Tel Aviv), reports to the CEO
- **Daniel Weiss**, VP Sales (Tel Aviv), reports to the CEO
- **Shira Levin**, VP Customer Support (Tel Aviv), reports to the CEO
- **Orly Peretz**, VP People (Tel Aviv), reports to the CEO
- **Avi Mizrahi**, CFO (Tel Aviv), reports to the CEO

## Engineering (reports to Michal Ben-David)

- Core API team: lead Itai Barak, 7 people. Owns flow-api.
- Engine team: lead Dana Levi, 8 people. Owns flow-engine and flow-scheduler, and runs the Kestrel project.
- Integrations team: lead Omer Haddad, 7 people. Owns connectors-hub and notifications.
- Billing team: lead Rina Sasson, 6 people. Owns billing-service.
- Identity team: lead Gil Avraham, 5 people. Owns auth-service.
- Frontend team: lead Maya Rosen, 7 people. Owns web-app.
- Data team: lead Eitan Klein, 6 people. Owns lighthouse and the data pipelines.
- Platform team: lead Boaz Nahum, 7 people. Owns the Kubernetes clusters, Conveyor, databases, Kafka and observability.

Engineering has 53 people in total in its eight teams, plus the VP Engineering.
