# Parental Leave Policy (effective April 2026)

This version replaces the January 2025 parental leave policy. It was announced at the January 2026 all-hands and applies to every child born or placed on or after April 1, 2026. Births before that date remain under the 2025 policy.

Acme provides the benefits below on top of the statutory entitlements. Where the law grants more, the law applies.

## Birth parent (Israel)

- Under Israeli law, the birth parent is entitled to 26 weeks of maternity leave, of which 15 weeks are paid by the National Insurance Institute (Bituach Leumi) for eligible employees.
- Acme adds 4 extra paid weeks immediately after the 15 statutory paid weeks, at full base salary. In total, the birth parent therefore has 19 paid weeks.
- Pension and study fund contributions continue during all paid weeks.

## Non-birth parent (Israel)

- The non-birth parent receives 15 paid days of leave from Acme (three working weeks), to be taken within the first 6 months after the birth or adoption.
- The days can be split into up to three blocks.

## Gradual return

Every parent returning from leave of 4 weeks or more may work 80% of their regular hours during the first month back, at full salary. Coordinate the schedule with your manager before you return.

## Adoption and surrogacy

Adoptive parents and parents through surrogacy receive the same benefits as birth and non-birth parents, starting from the date the child joins the family.

## Berlin employees

Employees of the Berlin office are covered by German parental leave law (Elternzeit and Elterngeld). Acme provides the same 15 paid days for the non-birth parent and the same gradual-return arrangement on top of the statutory entitlement.

## Process

1. Notify your manager and the People team at least 8 weeks before the expected leave date, or as soon as possible for adoption.
2. Submit the leave request in the HR portal, including the expected start date and, for the non-birth parent, the planned blocks.
3. The People team will contact you with the National Insurance Institute forms.

## Summary of changes from 2025

- Extra paid weeks for the birth parent: from 1 week to 4 weeks.
- Paid days for the non-birth parent: from 5 days to 15 days, and the window to use them from 3 months to 6 months.
- New: gradual return at 80% hours for the first month.
