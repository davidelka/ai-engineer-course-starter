# Procurement Policy (effective January 2025)

This policy applies to every purchase of goods or services from an external vendor that is paid by Acme directly (not reimbursed through expenses).

## Approval thresholds

Purchases are approved according to the total contract value for the first year, including VAT:

| Total value (USD) | Approver |
|---|---|
| up to $5,000 | direct manager |
| $5,000 to $25,000 | department VP |
| above $25,000 | CFO |

## Quotes

- For purchases above $10,000, at least 3 written quotes are required, or a written justification for choosing a single vendor.
- Repeat purchases from an existing vendor under an existing contract do not need new quotes.

## Software and SaaS vendors

New software or SaaS vendors that will hold Acme data must pass a vendor security review by the Security team before the contract is signed. Access to the tool for employees is then requested according to the SaaS Access Policy.

## Process

1. Submit a purchase request in NetSuite with the vendor, the amount, and the business purpose.
2. After approval, Procurement (Moshe Biton) issues a purchase order (PO) to the vendor.
3. Invoices are paid net 30 after the goods or services are received.

## Splitting purchases

Splitting a purchase into several smaller ones to avoid a higher approval level is not allowed and is treated as a policy violation.
