# Remote Work Policy (effective January 2025)

- Employees may work remotely 1 day per week, chosen together with the direct manager. Sunday to Thursday are office days by default.
- Remote work from abroad is not permitted, except during approved business travel.
- Acme provides a one-time home office allowance of 1,000 NIS, claimed through the expense system within 60 days of the purchase.
- Employees must be reachable on Slack during core hours, 10:00 to 16:00 Israel time.
- Teams may agree on additional remote days for specific periods (for example during a strike or extreme weather) with the approval of the department VP.
- This policy will be reviewed by the People team by the end of 2025, based on the results of the employee engagement survey.

## How to choose your remote day

Agree on a fixed remote day with your manager at the start of each quarter and mark it in your calendar as "WFH" so that colleagues can plan meetings. Managers should make sure that not all members of a small team choose the same day, so that someone from the team is always available in the office for customers and colleagues.

## Working from home safely

Work only on the company laptop, on a private network protected by a password. Do not work from public places with screens visible to others when handling customer information. If you need equipment for your home office, use the one-time allowance above; IT provides the laptop and monitor.

## Berlin office

Berlin employees follow this policy with Monday to Friday as office days by default.
