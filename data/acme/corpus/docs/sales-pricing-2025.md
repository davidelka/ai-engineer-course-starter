# Acme Flow Price List (effective January 1, 2025)

Prices are in US dollars per user per month, excluding VAT. Customers in the EU are invoiced in EUR at the rate published by Finance each quarter; customers in Israel may be invoiced in ILS.

## Plans

| Plan | Price per user/month (annual billing) | Price per user/month (monthly billing) | Included runs per month | Users |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $0 | $0 | 1,000 | up to 3 |
| Team | $10 | $12 | 10,000 | 3-100 |
| Business | $25 | $30 | 100,000 | 10-500 |
| Enterprise | custom | custom | custom | from 50 |

## Overage

- Team: $10 per additional 1,000 runs.
- Business: $7 per additional 1,000 runs.
- Starter workspaces stop running workflows when the monthly limit is reached until the next month or an upgrade.

## Features by plan

- Starter: 5 active workflows, community support.
- Team: unlimited workflows, email support, 2FA.
- Business: everything in Team, SAML single sign-on, audit log (1 year), 99.9% uptime SLA, priority support.
- Enterprise: everything in Business, 99.9% uptime SLA with higher credits, dedicated customer success manager, custom contract terms.

## Discounts

- Non-profit organizations and schools: 40% off Team and Business.
- Multi-year contracts (Enterprise only): up to 10% off, approved by the VP Sales.
