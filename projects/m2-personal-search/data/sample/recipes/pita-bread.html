Fluffy homemade pita bread

Makes 8 pitas. Ingredients: 500 g flour, 7 g dry yeast, 1 tsp sugar, 1.5 tsp salt, 330 ml warm water, 2 tbsp olive oil.

Knead for 8 minutes and let the dough rise for 1 hour. Divide into 8 balls and roll each to 5 mm.

Bake on a preheated stone or tray at 250°C for about 4 minutes, until the pitas puff up. Keep them covered with a towel so they stay soft.
