## Procedure: Laptop replacement
1. Open a ticket in the IT portal with the laptop's asset tag.
2. IT checks the warranty and, if needed, orders a replacement within 2 working days.
3. Back up local files to the company drive; the old laptop is wiped on return.
4. Return the old laptop within 7 days, or its value is deducted from the equipment budget.

## Procedure: Expense claim
1. Photograph the receipt on the day of the expense.
2. Submit it in Tally with a project code.
3. Your manager approves within 5 working days.
4. Payment arrives with the next monthly salary.

## Procedure: Onboarding week
1. Day 1: equipment pickup at reception at 09:00 and security training.
2. Day 2: meet your buddy and set up development tools.
3. Day 3: first small task merged.
4. Day 5: a 30-minute check-in with your manager and People Operations.

## Procedure: Offboarding
1. The manager files the departure in the HR portal at least 14 days ahead.
2. Access is removed at 18:00 on the last working day.
3. Equipment is returned to IT on the last day.
4. An exit interview is offered but optional.

## Procedure: Reporting a security incident
1. Do not try to investigate on your own.
2. Page Security Operations via #sec-911.
3. Write down what you saw and when.
4. Keep the affected device on and connected unless told otherwise.

## Procedure: Booking business travel
1. Get your manager's approval in the travel tool.
2. Book flights through the travel agency, economy class for flights under 6 hours.
3. Hotels must respect the per-diem lodging cap.
4. Share your itinerary in the travel tool for duty of care.

## Procedure: Requesting a training budget
1. Every employee has 4,000 NIS per year for courses and conferences.
2. Submit the course link and price in the HR portal.
3. Your manager approves; People Operations pays the provider directly.
4. Share a short summary with your team after the course.

## Procedure: Parental leave planning
1. Notify your manager and People Operations at least 30 days before the leave.
2. Agree on a handover document.
3. Your access stays active during the leave.
4. A return-to-work meeting is scheduled for the first week back.

## Procedure: Working from abroad
1. Up to 10 working days per year, with written approval from your department's VP.
2. Check the security checklist for the destination country.
3. Use the VPN at all times.
4. Log the days in the HR portal.

## Procedure: Equipment for home office
1. A one-time allowance of 1,500 NIS for chair, desk or screen.
2. Claim it in Tally within 60 days of purchase.
3. Items above 1,500 NIS need manager approval.
4. The equipment remains yours after 24 months.
